# Reliable UDP Acceptance Test – 5% Packet Loss, 0 ms Delay
  
**Direction:** H1 to H2  
**Packets attempted:** 20  
**Configuration:** 5% packet loss, 0 ms delay

## Sender Results

- Successfully acknowledged: 20
- Failed packets: 0
- Retransmissions: 8
- Success rate: 100%

## Receiver Results

- Unique DATA packets processed: 20
- Duplicate DATA packets detected: 4
- Duplicate ACK responses sent: 4
- Message IDs with duplicate DATA: 3002, 3004, 3008, 3017

## Observation

All 20 DATA messages were successfully acknowledged. H2 detected four duplicate DATA messages and sent ACK responses again without processing those messages a second time. The sender completed the transfer with eight retransmissions.

**Conclusion:** The test demonstrates successful reliable transfer and duplicate detection under simulated random packet loss. It does not independently verify a deliberately dropped final ACK or a deliberately selected DATA packet.
